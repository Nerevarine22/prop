Conduct a source-grounded research study of AceTrader using only the sources in this notebook. The result will be reviewed by a human before normalization or publication.

## Required sections

1. **Executive brief and operating model** — official positioning, legal characterization, complete trader lifecycle, simulation versus live execution, subscriptions versus one-time fees.
2. **Programs, pricing and eligibility** — every Evaluation and Instant Fund variant, account sizes, recurring or one-time cost, reset fees/credits, targets, MLL/drawdown type, daily limits, consistency, minimum days, duration, leverage, profit split and availability. Preserve every current tier.
3. **Payouts and trader compensation** — split, first-payout conditions, cadence, minimum, processing, token/network, gas/fees, KYC, closed-position requirements, partial withdrawals, balance/drawdown impact, pending restrictions and breach consequences.
4. **Trading environment** — platform, Hyperliquid/HIP-3 relationship, custody, wallet requirements, exact instruments, margin mode, order types, leverage, fees, funding, slippage, news/weekend trading, bots/API, copy trading, hedging, HFT/arbitrage/grid/martingale and position limits.
5. **Risk mechanics** — explain Maximum Loss Limit precisely, including trailing behavior, end-of-day high-water marks, caps, realized/unrealized PnL, resets and differences between Evaluation, Instant Fund and Trade Fund.
6. **Legal entity and availability** — entity, address, jurisdiction, registration number, founders/team, regulatory disclaimers, age, KYC stage, restricted countries, VPN/IP/device rules, governing law and document hierarchy.
7. **Rewards and ecosystem** — referrals, free trial, reset credits, Arena, community reward, points/token/airdrop status, competitions and promotions. Separate live mechanics from roadmap or marketing.
8. **Transparency and operating statistics** — every self-reported metric with date/source; audits, on-chain wallets, payout ledgers, proof of reserves and limitations. Never equate nominal funded allocations with treasury assets.
9. **Reviews and reputation** — dated ratings/counts only when supported; recurring positive/negative themes and company responses, separated from factual audit conclusions.
10. **Red flags and green flags** — concrete rule conflicts, legal discretion, stale docs, recurring fees, technical or verification risks, and real transparency signals with their limits.
11. **Sources, conflicts and unresolved questions** — list sources, preserve conflicts with page/date, identify canonical values only from hierarchy or freshness, and end with missing facts.
