# Research Brief: Dizso Funded Project Analysis

### 1. Project Overview and Operating Model
Dizso Funded is an emerging "onchain prop trading firm" currently in the final stages of pre-launch development. Positioned as a market disruptor, the project’s primary value proposition is the replacement of "outdated platforms" with a "faster, more transparent trading experience." This focus on transparency is a direct challenge to the "black box" execution models prevalent in traditional proprietary trading.

**Asset Coverage:**
The project intends to provide capital for institutional-grade trading across the following sectors:
*   Crypto
*   Forex
*   Commodities
*   Equities
*   Prediction Markets

**Current Status:**
As of the research ledger date (August 16, 2026), the project is "Still in development." Given the advertised launch window of "August 2026," the platform is effectively in an imminent launch phase or a critical transition period from development to live operations.

### 2. The Trader Lifecycle
*   **Onboarding Funnel:** Current entry is restricted to an "Early Access" phase. Lead generation is handled via an email capture form and a primary Discord community, indicating a community-centric growth strategy.
*   **Evaluation and Funding:** The specific protocols for trader evaluation—including stage counts, specific profit targets, and capital allocation tiers—are currently **Not Disclosed (ND)**. From an analyst's perspective, the absence of these mechanics prevents a full assessment of the firm's risk-to-reward ratio for participants.
*   **Trading and Execution:** Execution is intended to be entirely "onchain," managed through a unified dashboard. This infrastructure aims to provide "minimal fees" by bypassing traditional intermediary brokerage layers.
*   **Profit Attribution Model:** Documentation confirms that traders are eligible to retain **up to 80% of profits**.

### 3. Documented Offers, Programs, and Accounts
As of the current ledger date, the specific parameters of the trading "Challenges" remain unpublished.
*   **Affiliate Program:** An "Affiliates" link is explicitly present in the platform's navigation, confirming a multi-tiered growth model. However, specific commission structures or referral rewards are **Not Disclosed**.
*   **Account Tiers:** Sources such as "PricingCheckout" and the "Rulebook" are currently ND. No specific account sizes (e.g., $50k, $100k) or associated entry costs have been made public.

### 4. Technical Rule Mechanics (Drawdown and Profit Targets)
Critical risk parameters remain speculative as specific percentages and definitions are not yet documented.
*   **Daily Loss and Max Drawdown:** Not Disclosed in available source documentation.
*   **Profit Targets:** Not Disclosed in available source documentation.

Whether the firm utilizes a Balance-based or Equity-based drawdown (or a trailing drawdown model) will significantly impact the viability of the 80% profit split.

### 6. Trading Permissions and Restrictions
*   **Permissions:** While the platform markets a "one dashboard" approach, it does not explicitly confirm API access or support for automated bot trading.
*   **Prohibited Strategies:** There is no documented list of restricted behaviors (e.g., news trading, high-frequency trading, or copy trading). The absence of a formal FAQ or Rulebook at this stage leaves the operational boundaries of the firm undefined.

### 7. Execution and Infrastructure
*   **On-Chain Advantage:** Unlike traditional prop firms where trades are often simulated or executed in internal liquidity pools, "onchain" execution implies settlement on a public ledger, providing the "transparency" emphasized in the firm's marketing.
*   **Blockchain Integration:** The landing page features a "Built on" visual anchor intended to signify the host blockchain. However, this is currently a logo-only placeholder with no associated text-string identifier to confirm the specific network (e.g., Ethereum, Solana, or Layer 2).
*   **Platform Venue:** The project advocates for a "one dashboard" system for trading, tracking, and payouts, explicitly contrasting it with "outdated platforms."

### 8. Payout and Compensation Mechanics
*   **Eligibility and Cadence:** Payout timing, minimum thresholds, and processing windows are currently **Not Disclosed**.
*   **Settlement Path:** Payouts are linked to the on-chain model. While the specific settlement assets (e.g., USDC, USDT, or native tokens) are not documented, the model implies a non-custodial or smart-contract-based distribution.
