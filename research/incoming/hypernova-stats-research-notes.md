## Research interpretation

- Hypernova provides materially stronger payout traceability than firms publishing aggregate totals alone because recent payouts link to transaction-level Arbitrum evidence.
- A visible reserve balance is not the same as an independent audit, proof of liabilities or a solvency opinion.
- Funded AUM is a platform-defined account metric and should not automatically be treated as live capital deployed or treasury assets.
- Funnel, pass-rate and trading-volume figures should be labeled company-published unless independently reconstructed from raw data.
